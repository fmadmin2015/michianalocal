What is Michiana?

Michiana is the region straddling the Michigan-Indiana state line, anchored by South Bend and Mishawaka in Indiana and Niles and Buchanan in Michigan, and stretching out to Elkhart, Goshen, and St. Joseph County. It's not an official city or county; it's a shared identity for a region that's always crossed state lines to shop, work, and do business together.

Michianalocal exists to make that easier: one place to find a local business by category, see real reviews, and know you're supporting someone who actually lives here.
