Theta Ridge Coffee

Theta Ridge Coffee is a green coffee importer and roasting resource based in South Bend, supplying home roasting enthusiasts and wholesale clients with green coffee beans, roasting equipment and supplies, and hands-on classes.

About Theta Ridge Coffee

Theta Ridge Coffee is a key resource for Michiana's home and wholesale roasting community, based in South Bend. The business imports green coffee beans and supplies the equipment, roasters, and classes that both beginners and experienced roasters need to refine their craft.

Services

  • Green coffee bean imports
  • Home roasting equipment & supplies
  • Wholesale coffee supply
  • Roasting classes for beginners and experienced roasters

Service area

Theta Ridge Coffee serves home roasters and wholesale clients from its South Bend location, across the greater Michiana area.

Frequently asked questions

Does Theta Ridge Coffee offer classes for beginners?

Yes. Theta Ridge Coffee offers classes and equipment for those new to home coffee roasting, as well as more experienced roasters looking to refine their skills.

Does Theta Ridge Coffee supply wholesale clients?

Yes. Theta Ridge Coffee imports green coffee for both home roasting enthusiasts and wholesale clients.

Where is Theta Ridge Coffee located?

Theta Ridge Coffee is located at 6879 Enterprise Dr Ste 200, South Bend, IN 46628.
